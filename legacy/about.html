So, about me ⛅

I'm an experience designer grounded in UX and service design. I like opening a problem up properly — enough user research to find what's actually driving it — before spending time building the wrong solution.

My immersive work has focused on experiences that aren't obsessively reliant on screens and headsets. I'd rather use sensemaking principles to work across all of someone's senses and cognitive processes to build a deeper kind of immersion. I'm particularly interested in narrative design: how it makes levels in games and immersive experiences that people come back to, and how it doubles as a tool for examining wicked problems — especially alongside modelling and simulation.

These days a lot of that lands somewhere less expected. At Lush I've been piloting point-of-sale and stock systems across the region, building training that sticks, and automating the boring parts so the people on the floor can get on with the actual work. It turns out “design a system humans can live inside” is the same problem whether it's an immersive experience or a Tuesday afternoon in retail.

I like working in teams — taking everyone's strengths, weaknesses and biases into account to build something that genuinely analyses and solves problems together.
