The folio I put together at the end of the Masters. It's a container rather than a piece of work in its own right — a professional bio, a map of the skill set, and excerpts from things that now live in this archive as their own pages.

What's in it:

- **A professional bio and skills map.** The map branches from experience design and immersive design out into design thinking, user research, narrative design, sensemaking and prototyping. It's a 2023 snapshot and it hasn't aged well, which is why it lives in here rather than on the about page.
- **The HIV and PrEP work** — see [the wicked problem of HIV prevention](/work/wicked-problem-hiv) and [the PrEP subsidy model](/work/prep-subsidy-model).
- **The *sun stealer* narrative arch** — the full game design document is [here](/work/sun-stealer).
- **An excerpt from *superposition \\ true ch'ien*** — the whole essay is [here](/work/superposition).
- **A poem called *Salt*.** This is the only thing in the folio that doesn't appear anywhere else on the site. It's about kindness arriving where you expected dread — a horror-film build-up that resolves into someone's arms rather than a knife.

Since most of it now has a page of its own, treat this as a snapshot of how things looked in 2023 rather than the place to start.
