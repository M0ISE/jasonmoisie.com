The player wakes early, dresses, takes their grandmother's camera bag and heads for the beach. At the cliff they choose a path. Two lead down into the game; one leads out of the time loop entirely, into breakfast with a kind stranger who turns out to be both the player and their grandmother — an ending you can reach in the first five minutes if you talk to the right person.

Take the other path and the world gets *darker* instead of lighter. Dawn doesn't break. Something is following you.

The design document works through the narrative arch level by level — exposition, rising action, and the branching structure underneath. Time, in the game's own words, is like layers of paint.
