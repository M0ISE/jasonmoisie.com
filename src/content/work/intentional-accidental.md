A piece about what survives translation.

An object made in clay is scanned, reconstructed digitally, and then returned to clay again. Each pass through a medium introduces its own accidents — the scanner misreads a shadow, the mesh smooths a thumbprint, the second firing warps something that was square. The question is which of those accidents are noise and which are the actual work.
