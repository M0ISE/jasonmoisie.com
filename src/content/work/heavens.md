*The Thing From The Future* is an imagination game: you're dealt a future — its timeframe, its terrain, its mood — and you have to describe an object that could only exist there.

'heavens' is what came out of one of those hands. The value isn't the object; it's that describing a thing forces you to commit to the world around it in a way that abstract forecasting never does. You can't design the artefact without accidentally deciding what happened.
