*HER* builds a speculative future that's unusually careful: warm, high-waisted, softly lit, and almost entirely free of the dystopian signalling that normally does this work. That restraint is what makes it useful. The technology in the film isn't menacing — it's *accommodating*, and it's accommodating a problem it can't actually solve.

The essay uses that to ask a question worth asking of any designed system: what happens when technology addresses the symptom of a human problem so effectively that nobody has to address the problem?
